# Temporal Governed Analysis — Guardian Boundary

Temporal Governed Analysis (TGA) makes a rule- or law-based analysis contestable by preserving the exact represented source/time window, observation encoding, governing context, evaluation path, uncertainty, and provenance behind a conclusion.

## Contestability

A TGA result should be challengeable at the layer where disagreement actually exists: source integrity, observation, semantic mapping, rule selection, interpretation profile, enforcement profile, predicate evaluation, or counterfactual continuity.

**Canonical representation is not canonical reality.** A canonical record proves which representation was evaluated and how; it does not prove that the representation was correct.

Competing representations may remain visible when evidence does not resolve the disagreement. `UNRESOLVED` and `CONTRADICTORY` are legitimate states and must not be forced into a binary verdict.

## Temporal and counterfactual boundary

A counterfactual rule projection must identify the selected rule/law version and remain distinct from the framework that actually governed the historical event. Counterfactual analysis cannot rewrite historical applicability or fabricate downstream actions after state divergence.

## Guardian boundary

TGA documentation, publication, validation, visibility, reconstruction, or evidence matching does not create Guardian enforcement, override, execution, custody, standing, admissibility, legal, or adjudicative authority.

The voice or presentation layer is a renderer of governed records. Narration is not canonical evidence and cannot independently adjudicate a disputed event.
